Connect to Ollama

This site runs entirely in your browser and talks to Ollama on your own computer. Nothing you say is sent to any server.

First-time setup

  1. Install Ollama from ollama.com/download.
  2. Download a model. A good one for interviews: 
    ollama pull llama3.2:3b
    Have a stronger computer? Try llama3.1:8b, qwen2.5:7b, or gemma3:4b.
  3. Allow this website to talk to Ollama. Ollama blocks websites by default, so tell it this site is OK, then quit and reopen Ollama:
  4. Use Chrome, Edge, or Firefox. If the browser asks to allow access to devices on your local network, click Allow. (Safari blocks websites from reaching local apps.)
  5. Come back here and press Test connection.
